How it works

From everyday savings to mortgage progress.

The customer remains in control. We connect opportunities to save with the information they need to understand what those savings could mean for their mortgage.

01 / Find

Discover savings

Compare eligible household bills or shop through participating rewards partners.

02 / Confirm

Know what's real

Partner systems validate rewards. Bill savings are estimates until the customer actually switches.

03 / Choose

Stay in control

Customer chooses accessible savings or explores an eligible mortgage overpayment.

04 / See

Visualise progress

The Home Time Machine models how an extra monthly amount could affect their mortgage.

An everyday example

Someone finds a possible £25 monthly bill saving and expects £10 a month from eligible shopping rewards. They can explore using the combined amount to save for a goal or overpay their mortgage.

Technical concept

What would actually need to connect?

Open Banking can support consented account information; it does not, by itself, provide the details of every mortgage or permission to move money.

Illustrative partner architecture
Account dataCustomer-consented Open Banking via an appropriately authorised provider
Offers & rewardsRetailer, affiliate, voucher and comparison partner feeds; rewards verification
Mortgage & paymentsParticipating lender APIs or secure lender hand-offs; appropriate payment authorisation
These services feed a Home Time experience where customers see their position, choose an action and explore scenarios. No such systems are connected in this prototype.

Security & permissions

Customer consent, data minimisation, appropriate authentication, contractual permissions, security testing and data retention controls are essential.

Mortgage accuracy

Lender-specific rates, overpayment allowances, early repayment charges and payment status must be verified, not assumed.

Rewards operations

Eligibility, attribution, funding, exclusions, refunds, cancellations and reward reversals need agreed processes.

Regulated services

Payments, Open Banking, financial promotions and any credit or instalment offering require applicable regulatory and legal review, and appropriately authorised providers where needed.
