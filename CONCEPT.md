# Home Time — The Idea, the Problem and the Opportunity

**Hackathon concept | October 2026**  
**Live prototype:** https://lillycarroll2021-cyber.github.io/home-time-machine/  
**Technical PRD:** https://github.com/lillycarroll2021-cyber/home-time-machine/blob/main/PRD.md

## Why I started exploring this

We spend a lot of time helping customers *get* a mortgage or *switch* a mortgage. But what happens during all the years in between?

For most homeowners, the mortgage becomes a direct debit and a date in the diary. Yet their relationship with their home is anything but static. They pay down debt, think about renovating, wonder whether they could move, and try to make their money go further.

**The question behind Home Time:** What if a mortgage experience helped people make progress throughout homeownership, rather than only asking them to engage when their deal is ending?

## The problem

Today's mortgage experiences can feel fragmented and transactional.

- **Progress is invisible.** Customers see a balance, but not always a compelling picture of what they've achieved or what they could achieve.
- **Decisions are difficult to picture.** The effect of paying an extra £50 or £100 each month is hard to translate into a real future.
- **Everyday finances feel separate.** Shopping rewards, household savings and mortgages rarely connect in a way customers can understand.
- **Support arrives at isolated moments.** Renewal, moving and borrowing more can feel like separate products rather than chapters of one journey.
- **Loyalty is difficult to feel.** Customers need to see clear value from staying engaged, not just a claim that they're valued.

These are hypotheses informed by the product opportunity, not findings from a completed research study. Testing them is part of the proposal.

## What is Home Time?

**Home Time is a homeownership companion that helps people make the most of owning a home, whatever comes next. It connects mortgage progress, future decisions and meaningful life goals, with everyday savings and rewards as supporting tools.**

It's designed to feel more like a useful everyday consumer app than a traditional mortgage servicing journey.

A signature experience is the **Home Time Machine**, a visual tool where people can change a payment amount and see how their mortgage balance, interest and projected mortgage-free date could change.

Around that are four connected experiences:

**My Home:** See today's mortgage position, equity and key upcoming dates.

**Earn:** Explore whether rewards, vouchers and cashback from normal spending could become a repeatable contribution towards a mortgage goal.

**Pay Off:** Understand extra payments, potential interest savings and what to check with the lender before paying more.

**My Future:** Explore moving, renovations, switching deals or becoming mortgage-free sooner.

## A simple customer story

Imagine two homeowners with a mortgage balance of £200,000. They are wondering whether an extra £100 each month is worthwhile.

Instead of reading a dense calculator output, they open Home Time Machine and see two paths through the years. They can immediately understand the difference between their existing payment plan and one with an overpayment.

They then explore whether small, repeatable rewards or household savings could contribute to that monthly amount. They can save a scenario and come back to it when planning their next deal or a future move.

The prototype uses fictional people and figures. Real lending outcomes would depend on the actual mortgage contract, rates, restrictions and charges.

## What makes it different?

**1. It turns a balance into a story.** Clear visual progress makes a long-term financial commitment feel understandable.

**2. It connects everyday behaviour to long-term goals.** The rewards idea is not just collecting points. It is testing whether recurring small amounts could support meaningful action.

**3. It thinks beyond renewal.** Mortgage engagement could become a continuous, helpful relationship through the customer’s changing life.

**4. It creates a reason to return.** Saved scenarios, home milestones and relevant nudges could bring people back when something matters to them.

**5. It makes complex information more accessible.** Specific outcomes, clear language, interactive controls and inclusive design are foundational.

## Why might a bank care?

Home Time could help mortgage providers explore:

- Better customer understanding and confidence before important decisions.
- More relevant engagement between application and retention moments.
- Stronger retention and loyalty through demonstrated customer value.
- Appropriate opportunities to connect other financial services to actual homeownership needs.
- Better insight into customer goals, subject to clear consent and responsible data use.

**These are opportunities to test, not claims of proven impact or revenue.**

## What exists today?

A live, mobile-friendly hackathon prototype with:
- An interactive mortgage Time Machine and balance comparison.
- A fictional rewards experience and recurring cashback exploration.
- A home snapshot and mortgage milestones.
- Overpayment education and a payment preview.
- Goal-based future planning and saved scenarios.
- An illustrative renovation budget planner.

## What could come next?

**Near term:** Improve the visual experience, test comprehension and usefulness with homeowners, validate calculations and decide what belongs in a real MVP.

**Later, subject to partners and approvals:** Permission-based connections to banking and mortgage information; lender-specific overpayment support; genuine retailer rewards and cashback; and an optional home-improvement financing experience delivered by an appropriately authorised provider.

There are no live banks, retailers, credit services or reward transfers connected to the hackathon prototype.

## Updated distinction and positioning (October 2026)

**Home Time is not primarily an overpayment or cashback app.** Its focus is helping people navigate the complete homeownership journey: understanding progress, preparing for mortgage renewal, considering a move, planning improvements and exploring becoming mortgage-free. Shopping rewards and bill savings are optional ways to help fund customers’ chosen goals.

**Proposed USP:** *The homeownership app that grows with you.*

The next product discovery priority is to make the Time Machine compare multiple paths (stay, move, renovate and pay off) with transparent customer-specific data and trade-offs, rather than treating every goal as an overpayment scenario. The current prototype demonstrates a direction; those deeper comparisons are not implemented yet.
