For potential partners

Make everyday spending mean more.

Home Time is exploring how retailers, reward platforms and financial providers could help homeowners turn everyday savings into progress towards meaningful home goals.

Partnership concept only. No retailer, bank or rewards provider shown in the prototype is an existing partner.

The opportunity

Make rewards feel useful after checkout.

A customer earns £4 from an eligible home-improvement purchase or saves £15 each month on a household bill. Home Time could help them decide where that money goes — into accessible savings or, where appropriate, towards their mortgage — and show the longer-term effect.

Integration concept

What a customer journey could look like

1. DiscoverCustomer finds an eligible reward or potential bill saving.
2. QualifyPartner confirms offer terms, eligibility and reward tracking.
3. EarnConfirmed reward appears in the customer's wallet, subject to reversals and terms.
4. ChooseCustomer decides whether to save, spend or explore a mortgage overpayment.
Potential integration capabilities

What we'd need to make it real

  • Product feed or offer API, including current terms and eligibility.
  • Secure identity and attribution, fraud controls, reward validation and reversals.
  • Commercial arrangements for cashback funding, reporting and partner fees.
  • Approved consent, data protection and security controls.
  • Where applicable, authorised Open Banking and lender integrations for accurate mortgage information or payment hand-offs.
  • Regulatory review to ensure educational tools do not become unauthorised financial recommendations.
