CONCEPT OVERVIEW · OCTOBER 2026

Your mortgage.
A better future.

Home Time is an early-stage concept for helping UK homeowners see, earn and act on opportunities to make progress with their mortgage.

For discussion only · Not an offer to invest
01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Homeowners have information. They need momentum.

Mortgage experiences tend to centre on transactions, balances and renewal dates. Home Time explores what happens when those moments become an ongoing relationship: a clear view of progress, practical ways to save and simple scenario tools.

02 / THE PRODUCT

Time Machine

Turn financial scenarios into a visual picture: when might a mortgage be paid off, how does the balance change and what assumptions matter?

Earn

Explore a future rewards ecosystem where eligible partner cashback could be directed towards savings or mortgage progress.

My Home & My Future

Place key dates, equity estimates, moving plans and renovation needs into one understandable journey.

Connected home

With customer consent and appropriate partnerships, securely connect account and mortgage data to reduce manual work.

03 / BUSINESS MODEL HYPOTHESES

Potential commercial routes to validate

Partner-funded eligible cashback, premium planning tools, and bank or lender distribution partnerships are hypotheses only. Unit economics, conversion, customer acquisition, reward funding and revenue sharing must be proven through research and regulated partner review.

04 / INTEGRATION CONCEPTS

Designed to partner, not pretend to be a bank.

Bank connectivity: explore Open Banking through an appropriately authorised provider and explicit customer permission. Mortgage servicing: lender or servicing APIs, where available and contractually approved. Flexible renovation spending: an optional instalment-planning concept inspired by consumer payment experiences, with any actual credit subject to a licensed partner, affordability checks, legal disclosures and approval.

05 / WHAT NEEDS TESTING

Desirability

Will people return to an ongoing homeownership companion rather than visit only near renewal?

Understanding

Do visual scenarios improve comprehension and confidence without drifting into regulated advice?

Economics

Can rewards create meaningful financial outcomes without costs exceeding value?

Feasibility

Which integrations can be implemented securely and compliantly, and with whose consent?

06 / NEXT STEPS

From prototype to evidence.

Run structured usability sessions, test the rewards proposition with real numbers, obtain specialist regulatory input, explore permitted lender/technology partnerships, then define a limited MVP and a measurable pilot.

Partnership conversation: Looking to speak with banking, mortgage servicing, rewards and fintech partners about discovery and proof-of-concept opportunities. No funding amount or investment terms are proposed in this document.
